### Information Is Provided "As Is"

This site aggregates river level, flow, gauge, and temperature data from public
government sources (USGS, NOAA, USACE, USBR, state agencies, and others) and
publishes reach descriptions contributed by volunteer paddlers. All information
is provided **"as is" and without warranty of any kind**, either express or
implied, including but not limited to warranties of accuracy, completeness,
timeliness, fitness for a particular purpose, or non-infringement.

Gauge readings may be **delayed, incorrect, missing, or misinterpreted**.
Sensors fail. Upstream stations may not reflect conditions at your put-in.
Guidebook entries, class ratings, recommended flow ranges, hazard notes, and map
geometry may be **inaccurate, outdated, or wrong**. Rivers change — log jams
form, rapids wash out, dams spill, weather shifts, landowners revoke access — and
the site may not reflect current reality.

### You Are Responsible for Your Own Safety

Before you get on the water, it is **your responsibility** to:

- Verify river levels, weather, and hazards through multiple independent sources,
  not just this site.
- Scout unfamiliar rapids and know your own and your group's skill limits.
- Carry and know how to use appropriate safety equipment (PFD, helmet, throw bag,
  first-aid kit, communication).
- Obtain any required permits and respect public and private land access rules.
- Make the final go / no-go decision yourself — based on what you see on the
  river that day, not what a website said the day before.

If you are unsure, **stay off the water**. No gauge reading, guidebook
description, or trip report on this site overrides your on-the-water judgment.
